**Assessment A: design review.** Read-only. I looked at all 38 screenshots (cropping the tall phone captures to read them) and skimmed the dialog and RSVP source. I did not look at any detector output.

## 1. Design-specificity verdict
**It looks like any CRM; the data underneath is the only part that's clearly this product.** The organizer screens are clean and quiet: neutral surfaces, Geist type, a restrained brown accent and numbers in tables. But with the data swapped out they could be an agency CRM. What makes this product different (households, places, family allowances, several events) shows up only as data. The layout doesn't reflect any of it:
- The allowance is a muted 14px sentence.
- Each event dashboard is a generic summary strip plus tabs.
- The wedding-wide guest list is a generic data grid.

The guest envelope is the one authored moment, but a cream envelope with a gold wax seal is a common digital-invitation trope. The Weddings and System activity pages use an older visual language (serif display headings, small uppercase labels above titles, cards inside cards, pink Remove buttons). That split is what makes the app still feel assembled.

## 3. Cognitive load
**Three of eight checklist items fail: moderate load.**
- **Single focus:** on the event Guests tab, the header, a four-figure strip, tabs, the allowance line, search, five filter chips, Filters, Export, Import and Add household all come before the first row.
- **Minimal choices:** see the list below.
- **Visual hierarchy:** the five "Needs attention" rows on Overview look the same; an overdue payment is marked only by an amber dot.

Decision points with more than 4 options:
- Event guests toolbar: 10 controls.
- Each member row in event Settings: 5 controls with two separate save buttons (Save and Set).
- Send invitations dialog: about 8 choices (preview picker, three channels, three copy buttons, mark all).
- System activity filters: 6 controls.
- Vendors filter tabs: 5.

## 4. Emotional journey
- **Organizer:** Overview is a calm, confident start. The low point is sending invitations: WhatsApp opens one household at a time, then the organizer has to notice a small secondary button to record it. The session ends with "did I mark them?", which is the worst possible ending for invite tracking.
- **Contributor:** Their event dashboard leads with four large figures for their subset, with no "your households" framing. The number they actually care about ("27 left") is grey text below. On the wedding-wide Guests page they see all 30 households with dashes. That reads as either broken or nosy, which goes against "restricted views feel complete".
- **Guest:** The envelope is a real high point. But a guest who has already replied has to break the seal again just to see their answer. After opening, the page stacks many scroll-revealed cards (date, time, countdown, deadline pill, form, itinerary) before and after the form. The "Check your reply" confirmation is reassuring.

## 5. Strengths
1. **Overview "Needs attention":** specific and operational ("2 households at Mehndi haven't been sent an invitation"), in exactly the voice PRODUCT.md asks for.
2. **Budget:** grouped by wedding and event, separates agreed prices from estimates, shows "Estimate · $18.00 × 88 invited", and labels overdue clearly. It's the most authored planning screen.
3. **Form behavior:** the household dialog projects places live; the RSVP stepper starts at 0 ("Tap + to choose") so a default never inflates the headcount; the invitation preview is shown as a WhatsApp bubble. Phone lists turn into readable stacked rows with a sensible bottom nav.

## 6. Priority issues
**[P1] The event dashboard header is repeated on every tab and dominates the phone screen.**
- **Why:** The four-figure strip appears on Guests, Invitation, Schedule and Settings. On a 390px phone, the tab content starts around 550px down. The Schedule tab then repeats date, time and venue in a third place.
- **Fix:**
  - Replace the strip with one line under the title: "61 of 120 coming · 23 of 30 replied · 4 waiting · closes Nov 19". Keep the full strip on Guests only.
  - Remove the Date / Time / Venue / Replies close card from the Schedule tab.

**[P1] The allowance, the product's core concept, is barely visible.**
- **Why:**
  - The contributor's "48 of 75 places allocated · 27 left" is small muted text under figures about other things.
  - The organizer Guests page has an unlabeled line: "Demo Omar · Reception (Walima): 48 of 75".
- **Fix:**
  - For contributors, make "27 places left of 75" the main figure in place of the summary strip, with a thin bar under it.
  - On phone, keep it pinned next to Add household.
  - For organizers, add a labeled "Allowances" row with one bar per contributor.

**[P1] Invite tracking depends on the organizer's memory (Send invitations dialog).**
- **Why:** Invited status is high-risk data, but recording it is a small `btn-sm`. Done closes the dialog without a warning. Email is offered as a channel even when it isn't set up.
- **Fix:**
  - After each WhatsApp open, ask inline: "Sent to The Khan Family? Mark invited / Skip", then move to the next household.
  - Stop Done with "3 opened, not marked. Mark them invited?"
  - Hide Email unless it's configured.

**[P2] Two visual languages (Weddings, System activity).**
- **Why:** Serif display headings, "Workspaces" / "Audit Trail" labels above titles, cards inside cards, pink filled Remove buttons, a green Active badge and a jargon-heavy explanation box. Weddings also shows the wrong "← Events" back link, and members are managed in two places.
- **Fix:** Rebuild both with `PageHeader` and plain list rows, move membership into Settings, delete the explanation box, and rename "workspace" to "wedding".

**[P2] Member rows in event Settings.**
- **Why:** Role select, checkbox, Save, places input and Set in one row. On phone each member becomes a card about 400px tall, and the "Can edit all households" label is around 11px grey.
- **Fix:** One row with name, role, and places, plus a single Save that appears only after a change. Fold the checkbox into a role option ("Contributor, all households").

**[P2] A returning guest replays the envelope.**
- **Why:** The reply is hidden behind the ceremony, and "Tap to open your invitation" is low-contrast grey on beige.
- **Fix:** When the household has already replied, skip the envelope and lead with "You replied · 3 coming". Darken the hint and the "FOR THE … FAMILY" line.

## 7. Persona red flags
- **Alex (power user):** no keyboard shortcuts; Record reply hidden in "⋯"; two saves per member row; the wedding switcher is a native select; the Events page has search, a deadline filter and Clear for just 3 events.
- **Sam (accessibility):** low-contrast envelope text; 11–12px grey metadata throughout; the RSVP choice relies on green or rose tint plus a tiny check; the script-font "Will you be attending?"; icon-only WhatsApp buttons in rows; `window.confirm` for "Mark all invited".
- **Casey (mobile):** Add household and Send invitations sit at the top or below the fold rather than within thumb reach; the WhatsApp glyph in each row is a tiny tap target; the Vendors filter tabs clip "Not going ahead".
- **An older relative contributing guests on a phone:**
  - They land on wedding-wide figures that aren't theirs.
  - They need four taps (Events → Reception → Guests) to reach their own list.
  - "Event Creator" appears under their name and "Organizer (default)" in role menus.
  - Party size is three tiny number fields, when the guest RSVP already has better steppers that could be reused.
  - Dashes on the shared list read as missing data.
  - Budget, Vendors and Settings sit in the nav whether or not they need them.

## 8. Minor observations
- The Schedule preview isn't sorted (Decor team setup at 5:00 PM is listed after 7:00 PM). It uses 12-hour times while the rest of the admin uses 24-hour, and nothing marks which items are private.
- The organizer and contributor views of the same 30 households say "238 places" and "186 places", with no "that you can see" qualifier.
- The Invitation tab shows "Preview RSVP" twice. The text under Sending explains how the system works, which goes against Principle 2.
- Event Settings shows an empty Activity filter with "No activity matches" before anything has been searched.
- On Users, the identical "D" avatar circles add nothing, and Delete sits right next to Deactivate.
- The login dropdown lists every account name to anyone who opens the page.

## 9. Questions
1. What if a contributor's whole app were one screen: "Reception · 27 places left", their households, and Add household?
2. Is Invitation really a separate tab, or just the "not invited" state of the guest list with a Send button?
3. Should "invited" be recorded the moment WhatsApp opens, with an undo, instead of trusting the organizer to come back and mark it?
